Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

The short version: Waypoint has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it.

What Waypoint stores

Everything you enter or record in Waypoint: food, water, weigh-ins, workouts, routines, exercise history, check-ins, photos you take in the app, the routes of cardio workouts, and your profile and goals (such as age, sex, height and targets).

On your device

This data is kept on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch for workouts recorded there.

In your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Waypoint syncs your data between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. Only you can read it. The developer cannot access it. It is covered by Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off at any time in the iOS Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Weigh-ins that Waypoint reads from Apple Health are not copied to iCloud by Waypoint. They stay on the device, and Apple Health syncs them itself.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Waypoint uses Apple Health as follows:

  • Reads: your age, sex, height and weight, so you do not have to type them in; weigh-ins from other apps and smart scales; active energy, so a day's calorie budget can grow with your activity; and heart rate during workouts.
  • Writes: the food, water, weigh-ins and workouts you log, including the heart rate and energy your Apple Watch measured during a workout.

Data from Apple Health is only used to provide Waypoint's features. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone else. You choose which types Waypoint can read and write, and you can change that at any time in the Health app.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and nutrition labels, to scan a meal, and to take a selfie after a workout if you choose to. Barcode, label and meal scanning happen on your device. Meal scan uses Apple's on-device intelligence, so the image is never uploaded. A photo you choose to keep is saved with your data, as described above.
  • Location: only while you are recording a run, ride or walk that you started, to draw your route and measure distance and pace. The route is saved with that workout.
  • Motion & Fitness: to count steps during a workout you are recording.
  • Notifications: for reminders and alerts such as the end of a rest timer. These are created on your device. Waypoint does not send marketing notifications.

Services outside Waypoint

Open Food Facts

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a non-profit food database, to look up the product. Nothing that identifies you is included beyond what any internet request carries, such as your IP address. Their handling of it is covered by the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Apple

Waypoint uses Apple services for iCloud sync, Apple Health and downloading exercise videos from the App Store. If you have chosen to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple provides anonymous crash reports to the developer.

What Waypoint does not do

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: deleting the app removes its data from that device.
  • In iCloud: in the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your storage, and delete Waypoint's data.
  • In Apple Health: in the Health app, open your profile, then Apps, then Waypoint, and delete the data it saved.

Children

Waypoint is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
