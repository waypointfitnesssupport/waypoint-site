Waypoint

Food, training and progress in one app, for iPhone and Apple Watch. What you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Food

Scan a barcode or a nutrition label, or just say what you ate. Calories and macros against targets set for your goal.

Training

Routines, supersets and a rest timer, on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Runs, rides and walks drawn on a map.

Progress

Weigh-ins, weekly check-ins with photos, and a plan with waypoints on the way to your goal.

Yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. Works with Apple Health and syncs through your own iCloud.
