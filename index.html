Food

Log a meal in seconds.

Scan a barcode or a nutrition label. Say what you ate in your own words. Or tick off what you usually have for dinner.

  • Calorie and macro targets worked out from your body, your activity and your plan
  • Protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar and water, every day
  • Saved meals and recipes, and quick add for when you only know the calories
  • A budget that grows with the energy you burn

Training

Every set, every rest.

Build your routines and set them across your week. Tick a set and the rest timer starts on its own.

  • Rep ranges, supersets, warm-ups and reps left in the tank
  • Hundreds of exercises, each with a video, steps and mistakes to avoid
  • Records and full history for every exercise
  • A plate calculator for your gym's plates
  • Runs, rides and walks, with the route on a map

Apple Watch

Leave your phone in your bag.

Run the whole workout from your wrist. See the next set, log your reps and weight, and rest between sets. Heart rate and energy are saved to Apple Health when you finish.

Plans

A goal, with waypoints on the way.

Lose weight, maintain or build muscle over 8, 12 or 16 weeks, or until you reach a weight you choose. Waypoints mark the milestones in between.

  • Your weight as a trend, so one heavy morning doesn't throw you
  • Weekly check-ins, with progress photos you can compare side by side
  • Add your own reward to any waypoint
  • A review when the plan ends: where you started and where you finished
