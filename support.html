Help with Waypoint

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Email waypoint.fitness.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working. You'll usually get a reply within a few days.

Common questions

My data isn't syncing between devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, and that iCloud is turned on for Waypoint: open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and check that Waypoint is allowed. Sync can take a few minutes, and is faster on Wi-Fi.

Waypoint isn't reading or writing Apple Health data

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Waypoint, and turn on the data types you want to share. Waypoint on Apple Watch asks for its own permission, separately from the iPhone.

Meal scan isn't available

Meal scan runs entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on, and iOS 27 or later. You can still scan a barcode or nutrition label, or search for a food.

Some exercises show a picture instead of a video

The most common exercises' videos come with the app. The rest download the first time you need them, so they need an internet connection once. On older iOS versions some exercises show a still image instead.

Rest timer notifications don't arrive during a Focus

Rest timer alerts are marked Time Sensitive. If a Focus is still silencing them, open Settings, then Notifications, then Waypoint, and make sure Time Sensitive Notifications is on.
